# Installed slim cannon set

Six sprites generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using SimpleCannonChoices.png as the style reference. Final 32x32 assets replace the six existing files in Assets/Resources/WeaponIcons, preserving their GUIDs so loadout prefabs and equipped visuals share them. Point filtering and no compression are preserved. Detached opaque fragments are discarded during cropping.

SmoothMouseFollow.rotateBody is disabled on the gameplay Player so aiming rotates its Aim child and the separately rendered cannon while leaving the stone tower still. The cannon visual center is 0.45 units forward of the tower origin, placing its rear block over the stone mounting surface.

No handheld grip, no trigger, no large visible ring, no feet or off-center protrusions. Small useful mechanical details only: one reinforcement band and one simple panel. Shape must read clearly at tiny size. Entire single connected silhouette centered on a SQUARE TRANSPARENT canvas with consistent small margin; do not fill canvas with unnecessary empty space.
